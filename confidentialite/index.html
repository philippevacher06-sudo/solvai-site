Privacy

SOLVAI's public website does not require an account and offers no public data collection form. However, certain external technical services are involved when pages are viewed.

Data controller and contact

The person responsible for correspondence sent to SOLVAI is Philippe Vacher, publisher of this personal project. For questions about your data or to exercise your rights: atelier@solvai.fr.

Emails sent to SOLVAI

If you email SOLVAI, your email address, message and any information you choose to include are used to reply and follow up. Processing is based on the legitimate interest in answering enquiries. Email is provided by OVHcloud (Zimbra). Messages are kept only as long as needed for correspondence, then deleted when no longer necessary, subject to applicable obligations.

The presence of a mailto: link does not send anything automatically: your usual email application opens only when you choose to contact SOLVAI.

GitHub Pages hosting

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. According to GitHub documentation, GitHub logs the IP addresses of GitHub Pages visitors for security purposes, whether or not they have a GitHub account. See the GitHub Privacy Statement ↗.

Cloudflare analytics

The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page visits and performance. According to the provider, its analytics beacon does not access cookies or browser local storage and does not use visitors' personal data for this purpose. Cloudflare documentation ↗.

External charts: optional loading

The website uses system fonts and does not load Google Fonts. On the Markets, Heatmap and Calendar pages, TradingView widgets are disabled by default. They load only when a visitor explicitly clicks the relevant display button.

This choice connects to TradingView and may transmit technical browsing information or enable tracking technologies under the provider's policies. Without clicking the button, TradingView is not loaded. The choice is not saved; widgets are disabled again on the next visit.

More information: TradingView Privacy Policy ↗. Links to Telegram, GitHub and other official resources open independent websites only when clicked.

Retention, recipients and transfers

Technical recipients may include GitHub as web host, OVHcloud as email provider and external services actually loaded by the visitor. Some providers are located outside the European Economic Area and apply their own data protection safeguards. SOLVAI does not sell messages sent to it.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, restrict certain processing or object to it by writing to atelier@solvai.fr. You may also contact the CNIL ↗.

Separate private utility

The personal utility US500 WIN Drive Sync, which connects the owner to their own Google Drive, has a separate privacy policy. It does not accept public registrations.
