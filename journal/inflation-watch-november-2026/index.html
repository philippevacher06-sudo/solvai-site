Why inflation data matter

Inflation surprises can alter the anticipated path of Federal Reserve policy. Those expectations feed into the Treasury yield curve and the valuation of companies, though the reaction is not mechanical.

Avoid reading headlines in isolation

The next scheduled FOMC decision meeting is December 8–9, not during November 9–13. Weekly inflation releases affect expectations; they are not themselves Fed decisions.
