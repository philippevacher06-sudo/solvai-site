Reuters reported a broad rebound in technology and semiconductor shares on Friday after a severe sell-off, while the Nasdaq still ended its fourth straight losing week. These observations do not establish why every individual stock moved.

Three interacting vulnerabilities

Technology valuations can be sensitive to discount rates, large capital-expenditure expectations and crowded positioning. Together, these forces can amplify otherwise ordinary changes in sentiment.

01

Valuation & yields

Higher discount rates can weigh disproportionately on long-dated growth expectations.

02

AI investment cycle

Large infrastructure spending raises questions about timing of future cash flow.

03

Index concentration

A small set of large names may dominate headline index movements.

What would make a rebound more convincing?

A recovery would be easier to interpret if market breadth improved and bond yields stopped unsettling valuations. Continued leadership from only one cluster would leave concentration risk in place.
