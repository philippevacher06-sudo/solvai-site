US markets weekly review: a sharp reset before inflation week

The S&P 500 and Nasdaq fell over November 2–6, even after Friday's technology rebound. A resilient U.S. jobs report, shifting Treasury yields and anxiety around AI-related valuations left a crucial question for November 9–13: stabilization, or another test of risk appetite?

Three scenarios, no crystal ball.

SCENARIO 1

Stabilization

Inflation moderates relative to expectations; bond yields stabilize; a broader group of shares participates in a recovery.

Look for breadth across sectors, not only a bounce in a few AI names.
SCENARIO 2

Inflation surprise

CPI or PPI exceeds market expectations, prompting a repricing of the expected interest-rate path.

Watch Treasury yields alongside the US500 rather than reading the index alone.
SCENARIO 3

Renewed risk reduction

Tech weakness spreads while investors reduce exposure after the week's steep drawdown.

Check whether volatility remains concentrated or broadens across the market.
Scenario analysis is conditional and illustrative. It is not a forecast, investment recommendation or live trading signal.
